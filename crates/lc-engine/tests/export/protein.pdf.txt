<b>What protein is</b>
Protein is built from amino acids (Nieminen, 2021a, p. 44). Kallio and Virtanen
(2019) measured how much of it Finnish adults eat.
<b>Kinds of protein</b>
Complete proteins contain all nine essential amino acids (<i>Protein requirements</i>,
n.d.; World Health Organization [WHO], 2007, para. 3). The requirement is set
per kilogram of body weight. (WHO, 2007)

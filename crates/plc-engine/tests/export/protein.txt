What protein is
===============

Protein is built from amino acids (Nieminen, 2021a, p. 44). Kallio and Virtanen (2019) measured how much of it Finnish adults eat.

Kinds of protein
================

Complete proteins contain all nine essential amino acids (Protein requirements, n.d.; World Health Organization [WHO], 2007, para. 3). The requirement is set per kilogram of body weight. (WHO, 2007)
